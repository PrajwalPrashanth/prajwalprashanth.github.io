InvestiVerse 2022–2025

It started personal: a tracker for his own portfolio and trades, wired to a broker feed. That grew into trading and algo experiments — a rules-based trading bot among them — and then into a portfolio management platform he was building, through many rebuilds.

2023portfolio platform
2024StockResearchGPT

At the GPT moment he rebuilt it as StockResearchGPT: a research assistant that reads a company's annual reports, concall transcripts and investor presentations, then answers an investor's plain question with citations — the debt plan, what management said about margins — so people decide instead of reading hundreds of pages. Launched publicly in September 2024. After it, a product that used prediction-market data as a live news signal: 100 signups, then paused.

Misfits Capital 2025

He built the automations that do an investment team's reading and start its writing. A daily feed pulls in 20+ newsletters and sites and condenses each down to its actual argument, ranked for what each person cares about. A transcript pipeline turns voice notes and meeting recordings into text, and a writing system turns those into LinkedIn thought-leadership drafts in each person's own voice, learning from their edits. Quality checks run before anything is shown.

BizSherpa.ai 2025–

Sales, go-to-market and marketing work once done by hand is now done by AI systems he built. An existing network becomes a prioritised outreach plan with a reason for each contact. Prospect lists arrive already researched and ranked. A company's public data becomes a read on it before the first call. Marketing outreach and proposals are drafted by AI and checked before a person sends them. A coaching product was rebuilt with AI. For the person doing sales and marketing, the research, the list-building and the first draft are already done — the job is to judge and send.

Jovian 2019–2021

Jovian made developer tools for deep learning, and Prajwal handled the library. As its second employee he became the top contributor to the open-source Jovian Python library — 400k+ downloads — leading the features that let people track, monitor and compare training experiments, plus the backend APIs behind them. He started the Jupyter Plugins project and led a team of five interns to extend that tracking into Jupyter Lab, and built the learning platform from the ground up (React and Next.js in front, Flask and Postgres behind). He taught on it too: teaching assistant and grader on the machine-learning courses, one now past 600k views, plus a two-month study group he ran himself.
